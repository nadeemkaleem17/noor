## 1. Why this document exists

The owner looked at the finished shop and found it average: the navigation bar does not list the categories, search is only an icon, and the pages feel plain next to the stores customers know. This document (1) says what the shop displays and why it falls short, (2) records the research behind the fixes, (3) lists what v2 must show and do, (4) breaks the work into tasks, and (5) says how every task is judged. **Nothing in M8 is built until the owner approves §9.**

## 2. What the shop displays today, and the root causes

Looked at with screenshots (phone 390 px, laptop 1280 px) and the accessibility tree, 2026-10-15.

| Area | Today | The shortfall |
|---|---|---|
| **Header and navigation** | Logo, two links (New, Sale), search icon, wishlist, cart. On a phone a drawer with the same two links | **Categories are missing.** Cause: the seed writes menu `main` with only New and Sale, and `lib/nav.js` adds the category tree only when that menu is empty. The header also ignores category children, although the API already returns the whole tree with images and counts (`catalog.listCategories`) |
| **Search** | An icon that opens a bare field; results are a page | No suggestions, no categories in results, no recent or popular searches, no "no results" help |
| **Listing (PLP)** | Filters (checkbox and chip), sort, "Load more", product cards with a second picture on hover, badges | No sub-category chips, no grid density choice, cards show no colour swatches, rating, fabric line or quick add, although the data (`colorways`, `rating`, `subtitle`) exists |
| **Product page (PDP)** | Gallery with zoom, size chips, size guide, quantity, add button, accordion, related, recently viewed, reviews, notify-me | No delivery estimate on the page (only a fixed list), no review distribution or fit summary shown (the API has `byStar` and `fit`), no sticky buy box on desktop |
| **Cart and checkout** | Drawer and page, promo code, one-page checkout, COD | No free-delivery progress, no "you may also like", trust points are text only |
| **Home** | Hero, trust strip, category tiles, new arrivals, promo, bestsellers, editorial, collections | Plain rhythm; no shop-by-occasion or fabric, no review highlights; the hero was unreadable until today's token fix |
| **Look** | Playfair headings, Inter text, gold accent, rounded cards | Generic spacing and hierarchy, little motion, **placeholder pictures generated by the seed (a grey gradient with the product name)**: the largest single reason it looks average |
| **Mobile** | Drawer menu, sticky buy bar on the PDP | No quick access to categories, search or cart from the thumb zone |

## 3. Research

Method: published guidelines (Baymard, W3C), open-source storefront code (Shopify Dawn, Vercel Commerce, Medusa starter), and the live sites of three leading Pakistani fashion brands. **Evidence quality is stated for each item; vendor blog numbers are not used as facts.**

### 3.1 Guidelines and articles

| Finding | Source | Confidence | What we do with it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hover drop-down ("mega") menus are the main navigation on 88% of top US e-commerce sites; small details mislead shoppers about the hierarchy; parent categories must be clickable (14% of sites make them labels only) | [Baymard, drop-down menu benchmark](https://baymard.com/homepage-and-category-usability/benchmark/page-types/drop-down-menu) | High (free summary of a paid study) | Mega menu with clickable parents and "View all" |
| Use the **disclosure** pattern (buttons with `aria-expanded`), not the `menu` role; Escape closes and returns focus; Tab moves through the panel | [W3C APG disclosure navigation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/examples/disclosure-navigation/) | High (standard) | Our menu follows it; hover only opens it after a short delay and never traps focus |
| Only 19% of sites get all autocomplete details right; its value is guiding shoppers to better queries and the right scope | [Baymard, autocomplete](https://baymard.com/blog/autocomplete-design) | High | Predictive search with categories as well as products; keyboard and screen-reader support |
| Size buttons beat drop-downs (28% of sites still use drop-downs; 63% do not use buttons); 39% of testers found late that their size was out of stock | [Baymard, size buttons](https://baymard.com/blog/use-buttons-for-size-selection) | High | We already use chips; add stock per size on the card and the page |
| Put the size filter near the top and expanded (90% of testers then filter by size first) | [Baymard, apparel filters](https://baymard.com/blog/apparel-put-size-filter-near-top-and-expand-for-sidebar-filtering) | High | Size first in the filter order; shown on the phone sheet too |
| Colour swatches should show on mobile list items (57% of sites do not); avoid quick views for spec-driven products; keep scroll position on return; "Load more" over endless scroll | [Baymard, product list collection](https://baymard.com/research-articles/collections/product-list) (titles only; the detail is paid) | Medium | Swatches on cards; quick add (a size chooser, not a quick-view overlay); we already keep scroll and use "Load more" |
| Product pages: show estimated shipping cost (43% of sites do not); "free shipping" must not live only in a banner (32%); human-model pictures (75% lack) and zoom (72% lack); a ratings distribution (43% lack) and a fit sub-score | [Baymard, current state of product page UX](https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-ecommerce-product-page-ux) | Medium-high | Delivery and fee line on the page; free-delivery progress; reviews summary |
| A bottom tab bar is preferred by users in small studies; the long tail still needs a drawer | [University of Waikato study](https://researchcommons.waikato.ac.nz/entities/publication/3fa280bc-9c78-4449-b83c-6a39e93ee191) and vendor case studies | **Low** (small and vendor studies, directional) | An experiment behind a setting, judged on our own numbers (§8.5) |
| A free-shipping progress bar plausibly lifts order value (goal-gradient effect); every number published is vendor-reported; the threshold matters more than the bar | [Ninety9 guide](https://ninety9.dev/blog/free-shipping-progress-bar-guide.html) | **Low** for the size, medium for the idea | Build it; measure on our own orders |
| A second picture on hover helps; skip it for single-image products; handle touch | [Fudge guide](https://www.fudge.ai/guides/how-to-add-a-hover-image-on-shopify-product-cards/) | Medium | We already do it; add touch behaviour |

### 3.2 Open-source code read

- **Shopify Dawn, the most used theme** ([header.liquid](https://github.com/Shopify/dawn/blob/main/sections/header.liquid), [predictive-search.js](https://github.com/Shopify/dawn/blob/main/assets/predictive-search.js), [mega menu](https://github.com/Shopify/dawn/blob/main/snippets/header-mega-menu.liquid), [card-product](https://github.com/Shopify/dawn/blob/main/snippets/card-product.liquid)): the logo is left or centre; desktop menu type is dropdown, mega or drawer; the header can hide on scroll down and return on scroll up; the cart count is hidden from screen readers and read through hidden text; predictive search uses arrow keys with `aria-activedescendant`, an `aria-selected` option list, a status line that announces the result count for about a second, a cache keyed by the normalised query, and aborts in-flight requests; the mega menu is a `<details>` per parent with child and grandchild link lists; the product card builds the link's accessible name from the title and the badge, shows sale and sold-out badges, a hover image, price with compare-at, rating stars with a text label and quick add. **Used as a pattern reference only; we write our own code.**
- **Vercel Commerce** ([repo](https://github.com/vercel/commerce)): server-rendered, optimistic cart; search with typeahead is an optional add-on. **Medusa Next.js starter** ([repo](https://github.com/medusajs/nextjs-starter-medusa)): product, collections, cart, checkout, account; deprecated in favour of another starter. Neither has a richer header than we planned; they confirm that the elite part is detail and content, not unusual architecture.

### 3.3 Pakistani fashion brands, observed 2026-10-15 (live sites, desktop 1440 px)

| Brand | What it does that we do not |
|---|---|
| **Khaadi** ([pk.khaadi.com](https://pk.khaadi.com)) | Announcement bar with a **Track** link; centred nav (Sale, New In, Winter Collection, Ready to Wear, Fabrics, Home, Accessories, Fragrances); icon group of search, wishlist, account, bag; **mega menu with columns per collection line (Essentials, Signature, Khaas, Chapter 2) and under each the garment type (1 piece, 2 piece, 3 piece, kurta, pants, shalwar, dupatta)**. Listing: breadcrumb, **round collection chips** above the grid, filter and sort controls, item count (1,977), **grid density switch (1 to 4 columns)**, heart on every card and a quick **ADD** control; large photography with no card borders; card line "Printed \| Light Khaddar" (technique and fabric), name, price, New and "70% OFF" badges. Home blocks: shipping charges ("starting from Rs. 130"), track your order, find stores |
| **Sapphire** ([pk.sapphireonline.pk](https://pk.sapphireonline.pk)) | Full-bleed hero under a **transparent header**, large logo, section tabs (Woman, Fragrances), a drawer holding the whole category tree |
| **Outfitters** ([outfitters.com.pk](https://outfitters.com.pk)) | Transparent header over the hero, **Men / Women / Juniors tabs**, inline "Search" field, full-bleed campaign hero |

Pattern across all three: **navigation is category-first with depth; photography carries the page; the chrome is quiet.** Our chrome is not louder, but it has neither the depth nor the photography.

## 4. Principles for v2

1. **Category-first navigation, visible at all times** (desktop bar, phone bottom bar experiment, drawer for the long tail).
2. **Photography leads.** The UI stays quiet; images are large, consistent (3:4) and the shop never shows a generated placeholder at launch.
3. **Every feature works without JavaScript first** (links and forms), and JavaScript adds the polish. The mega menu and search suggestions are enhancements; the category links are in the HTML.
4. **Accessible by construction**: disclosure pattern, `aria-activedescendant`, live regions, 44 px targets, visible focus, reduced motion respected.
5. **Fast by budget**: every new feature loads on first use (lazy chunk) or is HTML and CSS; the ceilings of DOC-06.10 §2 may rise only by the amounts in §7 and only with a measurement.
6. **Measure before and after.** M8-01 records today's numbers so "better" is a difference we can read.
7. English only at launch; nothing here switches Urdu on.

## 5. What v2 shows and does (requirements)

"Must" ships in M8; "Should" ships if time allows; "Later" is written down so it is not forgotten.

### 5.1 Header and navigation
- **Must** Top bar = announcement (exists) with a **Track order** link and the WhatsApp link on the right.
- **Must** Main navigation built from the category tree: `New in`, each top category (Women, Men, Kids, Footwear, Accessories) with a **mega menu** (columns = sub-categories with their children; a picture tile from the category `image`; "View all <category>"; clickable parent), collections (Eid edit), `Sale`. Menu `main` stays editable by staff; when it is empty the tree builds it (today's fallback, now with children). The seed stops writing a two-item `main` menu.
- **Must** Disclosure pattern; open on hover with a 150 ms intent delay and on click/Enter/Space; Escape closes and returns focus; the panel closes when focus leaves.
- **Must** Sticky header that hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up; the cart badge updates without a page load.
- **Must** Phone drawer: drill-down categories with pictures, Track order, WhatsApp, search field at the top.
- **Should** Transparent header over a full-bleed hero on the home page (contrast kept by a scrim), solid on scroll.
- **Should** Phone **bottom tab bar** (Home, Shop, Search, Wishlist, Cart) behind a store setting, off on the product page where the buy bar takes its place.
- **Later** Account area (the shop is guest-only by decision).

### 5.2 Search
- **Must** Search field in the header (desktop inline, phone full-width overlay) with **predictive suggestions**: up to 5 products (thumbnail, name, price), matching categories, matching collections, and "See all results for ..."; keyboard (arrows, Enter, Escape), `aria-activedescendant`, a live status line, request cancel and a cache by normalised query; 150 ms debounce.
- **Must** Empty field shows **recent searches** (this browser only) and **popular searches** (a short list staff keep in the store settings).
- **Must** Results page: category chips to narrow, "did you mean" using the synonyms table, and a **no-results page that helps** (popular categories, WhatsApp us).
- **Must** Works without JavaScript: a plain GET form to `/search`.

### 5.3 Listing
- **Must** Category header: name, short description, picture, **round sub-category chips** (Khaadi pattern), breadcrumb, item count.
- **Must** Sticky filter and sort bar; size filter first and open; applied-filter chips; grid density switch (phone 1 or 2; desktop 3 or 4), remembered.
- **Must** Product card v2: second picture on hover (touch: swipe dots), **colour swatches**, rating stars with count, the technique/fabric line (new `subtitle` in `ProductSummary`), "New", "-x%", "Only N left" and "Sold out" badges, heart, and **quick add** (opens a size chooser on the card; adds without leaving the page).
- **Should** "Shop by occasion" and "by fabric" collection chips from attributes.

### 5.4 Product page
- **Must** Desktop: thumbnails column, sticky buy box, breadcrumb; phone: swipe gallery with dots, sticky buy bar (exists).
- **Must** Size chips show **which sizes are out** and the low-stock line per size; size guide link next to them (exists).
- **Must** **Delivery block**: choose a city (remembered), see the fee, the delivery days and the free-delivery threshold for that city, from the shop configuration (no new endpoint); never only in the banner.
- **Must** Reviews: average, **star distribution**, fit summary, photo reviews first, sort (data: `byStar`, `fit`).
- **Must** Trust points with real content (Cash on delivery, 7-day returns, WhatsApp support), each linking to its policy.
- **Should** "Complete the look" from bundles, a shareable link and a WhatsApp share button.
- **Later** Video on the gallery (media module has no video yet).

### 5.5 Cart and checkout
- **Must** Cart drawer: **free-delivery progress** ("Add Rs 1,200 more for free delivery"), line editing, promo, "you may also like".
- **Must** Checkout: sticky order summary on desktop, trust line, errors as now (focus and count announced).
- **Later** Saved addresses (needs accounts).

### 5.6 Home
- **Must** Hero (exists, readable), **category circles** under it, new arrivals, bestsellers, collections, editorial, promo band, trust strip, **customer reviews highlight**.
- **Should** Shop by occasion, by fabric; a "Shop the look" block.

### 5.7 Look and feel (design system)
- **Must** A written type scale and spacing rhythm in `tokens.css` (heading, subhead, body, caption; 4 px base, section gaps), consistent 3:4 imagery, card without chrome, one primary button style, 44 px targets.
- **Must** Motion: 150 to 250 ms for menus and cards, route view transitions where the browser supports them, skeletons for deferred blocks, all disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Must** Photography brief (§9) because no UI work can fix generated placeholders.

**Performance rule for M8.** Every task states the JavaScript it adds. Allowed rises over today's gzip ceilings (233.6, 239.9, 261.5, 243.7, 245.2 kB) are: mega menu + search suggestions + quick add loaded on first use add **no more than 12 kB to any page's initial JavaScript** (the rest loads when used); the home page may rise by at most 6 kB for the circles and reviews block. A task that exceeds this must justify the figure in this document before merge.

**Per-task Definition of Done** (all of): the requirement text satisfied; a new or extended end-to-end test (including a no-JavaScript case where the feature has one); keyboard-only and axe pass; the screenshot baselines updated on purpose with the diff reviewed; `pnpm check` and the budget script pass; the plan and `CLAUDE.md` updated in the same change; the scorecard rows it moves re-scored.

## 8. How we judge it

### 8.1 Automated gates (every task)
`pnpm check`, the full end-to-end suite on a fresh database, `pnpm --filter @noor/storefront budget`, axe on every page the task touches, keyboard-only journeys, no-JavaScript journeys, and the visual baselines (§8.2).

### 8.2 Visual regression
`test:visual` (M8-01): Playwright screenshots of home, a category, a sub-category, search with results, no results, a product, the cart drawer, the cart, checkout with errors, an order page, the 404 and the open mega menu, at 390, 768 and 1280 px, fixed data and fixed clock. A task changes the baselines only on purpose and shows the before and after in its report. This is how today's "dark text on a dark hero" and "chat button under the buy bar" would have been caught.

### 8.4 Usability test (M8-10)
Five people who shop online in Pakistan, on their own phones, ten minutes each, think aloud. Tasks: (1) find a kurta for a wedding from the home page, (2) find it again by searching "lawn", (3) choose a size and see when it arrives to Lahore, (4) buy it with cash on delivery, (5) track the order. Record task success, time, wrong turns and the words they use for categories. **Pass: at least 4 of 5 finish all tasks without help, and nobody fails to find a category.** The words people use feed the category and menu names.

### 8.5 After launch
`web-vitals` (exists), plus three counted events kept without personal data: search used, menu used, quick add used; and the phone bottom bar experiment judged by its effect on pages per visit and orders for two weeks before it becomes the default.

## 9. Decisions the owner must make before M8 starts

1. **Approve this plan** (scope of §5, order of §7, the performance rule, the scorecard and the usability test).
2. **Photography.** Real product photography is the largest lever and is not software. Send me the brief you want, or approve the one I write in M8-10: 3:4, neutral background, front, back, detail, one on a model, consistent light; at least 4 pictures per product. Until then the demo pictures stay and the scorecard row V3 is zero.
3. **Phone bottom tab bar** and **transparent header over the hero**: build as options, off by default (recommended), or skip.
4. **Delivery fees and the free-delivery threshold.** Peers: Khaadi "starting from Rs. 130", Alkaram a flat Rs 100, Outfitters free from Rs 2,500, Dhanak from Rs 2,000, Junaid Jamshed from Rs 1,500; the seed has Rs 250 and 350 and free over Rs 5,000. The progress bar in the cart makes the threshold very visible, so choose it deliberately.
5. **Menu structure.** Proposed top level: New in, Women (Stitched, Unstitched, Bridal and Formals), Men, Kids, Footwear, Accessories, Collections, Sale. Tell me the categories you really sell.
